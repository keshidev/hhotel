# No-Show User Manual

## SECTION 1 — RECEPTIONIST
### How to Mark a Booking as No-Show

#### Overview
A No-Show is when a confirmed guest does not arrive on their check-in date and cannot be reached. This feature allows the receptionist to officially record the no-show after 6:00 PM on the check-in date.

#### When can you mark a No-Show?
- The booking status must be "Confirmed"
- The check-in date must be today or in the past
- The current time must be 6:00 PM or later
- You must have attempted to contact the guest first

#### Step-by-Step
1. Go to Front Desk -> Check-In in the sidebar.
2. Find the guest's booking card in the Expected Arrivals list.
3. The "Mark as No-Show" button appears to the left of "Complete Check-In".
   - Before 6:00 PM: the button is grayed out and shows a tooltip: "Available after 6:00 PM on the check-in date"
   - After 6:00 PM: the button becomes active.
4. Click "Mark as No-Show".
5. A confirmation modal will appear showing:
   - Guest name
   - Booking reference
   - Room
   - Check-in date
6. Check the box: "I have attempted to contact the guest and received no response" (required).
7. Click "Confirm No-Show".
8. The booking card will be removed from the Check-In list.
9. A notification will appear: "Booking marked as No-Show. Guest has been notified by email."

#### What happens after
- The booking status changes to "No-Show"
- The room is released back to Available
- The guest receives an email notification
- The action is recorded in the Audit Trail

#### Important Notes
- This action cannot be undone
- You must attempt to contact the guest before marking no-show
- No-Show cannot be marked before 6:00 PM on the check-in date

## SECTION 2 — ADMIN
### Viewing and Monitoring No-Show Bookings

#### Overview
Admins can view all no-show bookings across the system, monitor no-show rates in reports, and review audit trail entries for every no-show action performed.

#### Where to find No-Show bookings
1. Go to Admin Panel -> Reservations.
2. Use the Status filter dropdown and select "No-Show".
3. All bookings marked as no-show will be displayed.
4. Review booking entries and status breakdown for no-show records.

#### In Reports
- No-Show count appears in the Reservation Status breakdown.
- No-Show bookings are tracked separately from Cancelled bookings.
- Use the date range filter to see no-shows in a specific period.

#### In Audit Trail
- Every no-show action is logged with:
  - Who marked it (receptionist name + role)
  - Booking reference and guest name
  - Room number
  - Exact timestamp

#### Important Notes
- Admins cannot reverse a no-show status — it is permanent.
- No-show bookings remain in the system for record-keeping and are never deleted.
